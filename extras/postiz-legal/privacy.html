Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

1. Who is responsible for data

"Postiz Local" is a personally operated, self-hosted application. The operator of the application is the only person who uses it. There are no other users, customers or visitors, and no personal data is collected from the public.

2. What data is processed

  • Operator account data — an email address and a password (stored only as a salted hash) used to sign in to the application.
  • Connected platform tokens — OAuth access and refresh tokens granted by TikTok, YouTube, Facebook and Instagram so the application can publish on the operator's behalf.
  • Content — posts, images and videos the operator creates or schedules.
  • Analytics data — basic metrics (views, likes, comments, follower counts) returned by the connected platforms for the operator's own content.

3. Where data is stored

All application data is stored locally, on the operator's own computer, in containers running under Docker. No application data is sent to the operator's own servers operated by any third party, and no data is sold, rented or shared with advertisers.

4. Third-party platforms

Data is transmitted to TikTok, YouTube, Facebook and Instagram only when the operator publishes content or requests analytics, using the authorisations the operator granted. Each of those platforms processes data under its own privacy policy.

5. Cookies

The application uses a session cookie strictly to keep the operator signed in. No tracking or advertising cookies are set.

6. Retention

Data is retained for as long as the application is in use. The operator can delete content, disconnect a platform account, or delete the entire installation at any time, which removes all stored data.

7. Your rights

Because the only individual whose data is processed is the operator, the operator may at any time request access to, correction of, or deletion of that data simply by acting on it within the application, or by deleting the installation.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always published on this page with its revision date.
