Postiz

Schedule and publish your social media content in one place.

Postiz is an open-source social media scheduling tool. Connect your accounts once, compose a post, and publish it to every channel from a single calendar.

Connect your channels

Sign in with your social accounts through their official authorization screens. Postiz never sees your passwords.

Compose once, publish everywhere

Write a post a single time and deliver it to each connected platform, formatted the way each one expects.

Schedule from a calendar

Plan ahead on a visual calendar, or publish immediately when something is ready to go live.

How it works

  • Connect the social accounts you want to publish to.
  • Create a post and attach any images or video.
  • Choose the channels that should receive it.
  • Publish immediately or schedule it for later.

Your privacy

We only request the account permissions needed to publish on your behalf and to read back basic profile information. You can revoke access at any time from your account settings on each platform.
