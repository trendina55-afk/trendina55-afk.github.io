Postiz Local

A personal, self-hosted social media scheduling tool.

This site hosts the legal pages for a small, privately operated application used to schedule and publish content to a single user's own social media accounts (TikTok, YouTube, Facebook and Instagram).

What the app does

  • Connects to accounts that the same person owns and controls.
  • Schedules and publishes posts created by that person.
  • Reads basic analytics for the posts it publishes.
